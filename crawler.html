Disclosure & opt-out — ArshiResearchBot

ArshiResearchBot is an academic measurement crawler operated as part of a peer-reviewed research study on how websites declare policies toward automated and AI agents. The study surveys the deployment of public policy artefacts across the web.

What it does

  • Requests a small set of publicly available, well-known policy files: robots.txt, llms.txt, ai.txt, tdmrep.json, and similar declaration files.
  • Reads only these policy files to measure whether and how sites declare agent policies.
  • Honours your robots.txt: if you disallow ArshiResearchBot, it will not fetch.
  • Crawls politely — low request rates, well-spaced, one request per file.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store, index, or process page content, text, or media.
  • It does not attempt logins, forms, or access to non-public areas.
  • It does not sell, share, or republish any data; results are reported only as aggregate statistics.

How to opt out

To exclude your site from this study, do either of the following:

  • Add to your robots.txt:
    User-agent: ArshiResearchBot
    Disallow: /
  • Or email research@arshichadha.com with your domain, and it will be removed from the crawl list.

Who runs it
